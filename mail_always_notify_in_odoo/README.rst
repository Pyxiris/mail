Force notification of internal users via odoo inbox regardless of
whether their email was in To:/Cc:

Odoo avoids notifying internal users about inbound messages if their
email is already in the To or CC header. The assumption is that the user
will be notified via their email inbox and should not be notified again
of the same message in odoo.

However, some companies may desire to force their users to engage with
odoo-related mail exclusively via odoo in order to avoid situations
where the user replies directly to the customer alone via email without
CC'ing the odoo alias, thereby creating a situation where communication
is divided between odoo and email.

Usage
=====

No special configuration needed.

Note that on install and upgrade all internall users will be checked for
and coerced into ``mail.group_mail_notification_type_inbox``.
